My name is Ying Fu. I am a Ph.D. student at the [National University of Defense Technology (NUDT)](https://www.nudt.edu.cn/) since September 2022, under the supervision of [Prof. Xiangke Liao](https://www.cae.cn/cae/html/main/colys/68400379.html) and [Prof. Shanshan Li](https://leopard-lab.github.io/people.html). I received my master’s degree in Software Engineering from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/) in 2020, where I was advised by [Prof. Ming Gu](https://www.thss.tsinghua.edu.cn/faculty/guming.htm) and [Prof. Yu Jiang](https://sites.google.com/site/jiangyu198964/home), and my bachelor’s degree from [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT)](https://www.bupt.edu.cn/) in 2017. 


Prior to my Ph.D. studies, I worked as a Senior Software Engineer at Ant Group, Blockchain Platform Department. 


My current research focuses on software security and reliability, with an emphasis on fuzzing and differential testing for complex system software, particularly database management systems and system-level platforms.
